Delete your Spendly account

In the Android app

Open Spendly → Settings → Data & privacy → Delete account. Type DELETE, then re-enter your password or confirm with Google. This erases your account, cloud personal data, payment requests you created, and local SMS queues on that device.

If you cannot open the app

Email the grievance contact published in the Privacy Notice from the same address as your Spendly account and ask us to delete it. We aim to complete this within 7 days of verifying it is you.
