Spendly Privacy Notice

Version 1.0 — Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

Spendly is the Data Fiduciary for this app. This notice describes personal data processed to provide a personal finance (and optional nutrition) service. Fill in grievance contact details in the app config before Play Store listing. Have counsel review this text.

Personal data we process

Account identity (email, name, Google photo URL if you use Google Sign-In). Financial data you enter (transactions, accounts, UPI ID, investments, borrowings, vaults, splits). Nutrition/health data if you use that workspace. Android SMS is read on-device only if you opt in — raw SMS is not uploaded. Camera/photos for receipts stay on device. We do not collect location, contacts, or advertising IDs.

Purpose

Core: account, ledger sync, dashboards. Optional (separate consent): SMS automation, Nutrition AI via Google Gemini, local notifications. We do not sell data or use third-party ads.

Sharing and cross-border processing

Google Firebase hosts accounts and saved data (may be outside India). Google Gemini receives food text only with Nutrition AI consent. Payment request links are world-readable (amount, payee name, UPI ID) until you delete them. Vault/split members see shared items you add.

Retention

Until you delete the account or the purpose ends, unless law requires longer. Raw SMS is not stored in the cloud.

Your rights

Access, correction, erasure, withdraw optional consent, nominate a person, and raise a grievance. In the app: Settings → Data & privacy. Core consent withdrawal requires account deletion.

Children

Spendly is for people aged 18 or older.

Grievance

Contact details to be published by the Data Fiduciary. We aim to respond within 7 days. You may complain to the Data Protection Board of India (dpdpa.gov.in) if unresolved.
